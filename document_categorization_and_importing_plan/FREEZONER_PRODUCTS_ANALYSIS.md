## Product Naming Convention

### Prefixes Used:
- **[A]** = Accounts Team (Internal workflow tracking)
- **[O]** = Operations Team (Execution tracking)
- **[S]** = Sales Team (Customer-facing)

### Purpose:
Separates same service into different products for:
1. **Department-specific tracking**
2. **Role-based access control**
3. **Reporting by department**
4. **Commission calculation per role**

## Service Tracking Types

### 1. **new_workflow** (Most Common)
- Creates project with tasks
- Full workflow automation
- Document management
- Compliance tracking
- **Used in:** 200+ products
- **Mazagawy Equivalent:** Template-based project creation

### 2. **no** (Simple Services)
- No project/task creation
- Direct invoicing
- **Used in:** 40+ products (operational costs, fees)
- **Mazagawy Equivalent:** Service-only products

### 3. **task_in_project**
- Creates tasks in existing project
- **Used in:** 2 products (Corporate Bank Assistance, Visa)

### 4. **project_only**
- Creates project without predefined tasks
- **Used in:** 1 product (Corporate bank account)

### 5. **event**
- Event registration
- **Used in:** 1 product

## Recommended Mazagawy Product Structure

### Instead of Freezoner's 3-Product Approach:

**Single Product with Smart Configuration:**

```python
product_template:
  - name: "Business Formation - FZCO 1 Year"
  - service_tracking: "new_workflow"
  - project_template_id: template_business_formation
  - document_required_type_ids: [passport, emirates_id, business_plan, ...]
  - document_deliverable_type_ids: [trade_license, moa, aoa, ...]
  - compliance_required: True
  - default_checkpoints: [collect_docs, name_approval, license_submit, ...]
  - department_access: {
      'sales': 'read_write',
      'operations': 'execute',
      'accounts': 'review_approve'
    }
```

### Benefits:
- ✅ No product duplication
- ✅ Single source of truth
- ✅ Easier maintenance
- ✅ Cleaner reporting
- ✅ Department tracking via project stages
- ✅ Flexible workflow
